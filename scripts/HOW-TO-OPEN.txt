Universal Converter: install and first launch
=============================================

1. Drag "Universal Converter" onto the Applications folder.

2. Open it from Applications. Because this app isn't from the App Store or
   signed by an Apple-registered developer, macOS blocks it the first time:

   - Click "Done" on the warning (don't move it to the Trash).
   - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
     "Open Anyway" next to "Universal Converter was blocked". Confirm with
     your password or Touch ID.

   (On macOS 14 you can instead right-click the app > Open > Open.)

   Terminal alternative:
     xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Universal Converter.app"

3. Use it:
   - Start dragging files in Finder, hold Shift, and drop onto a format in
     the ring that appears around the file. Hover "More" for every format.
   - Or drop files on the app window / Dock icon and click a format.
   - Right-click any format in the window to add it to the Shift-drag ring.

Everything converts on your Mac; nothing is uploaded.
Requires macOS 14 or later, on Apple Silicon or Intel.

Optional extras: installing Homebrew's ffmpeg and webp packages
(brew install ffmpeg webp) adds MP3, OGG, Opus, WebM, MKV, AVI and WebP.
